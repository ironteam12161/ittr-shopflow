ITTR v24.6.4 LABOR-CENTRIC INVOICE AUDIT

Design research direction
- Fullbay: estimate/service order/invoice reuses entered data and separates service, parts and labor hours.
- Mitchell Manager SE Job View: groups labor tasks and related parts into logical job containers.

Architecture implemented
- customer_invoice_lines.parent_line_id added non-destructively.
- Top-level labor line = repair operation.
- Part/sublet/fee child lines reference the labor line through parent_line_id.
- Server validates parent belongs to same invoice and is a labor line.
- Child line inherits job UID/name from parent labor server-side.
- Updating labor job name/UID propagates to child charges.
- Deleting labor deletes attached children first in same DB transaction.
- Work Order and Service Order invoice conversion create labor first and attach task parts to it.
- Startup reconciliation links legacy non-labor invoice lines to the nearest matching labor when possible.
- Invoice PDF prints labor then its attached charges.
