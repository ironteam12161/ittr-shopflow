ITTR ShopFlow v23.5 — Historical Productivity + Free AI
Build: ITTR-23.5-PRODUCTIVITY-FREE-AI-20260911

NEW / CHANGED
1. Historical Mechanic Productivity
   - Admin Operations Center > Productivity now supports Day / Week / Month.
   - Date navigation: Previous / Today / Next.
   - Optional single-mechanic filter.
   - Uses authoritative PostgreSQL task_time_sessions, not only today's work-order snapshot.
   - Splits sessions correctly across calendar-day boundaries in the browser's local timezone.
   - Shows recorded repair labor, unique tasks, completed time sessions, units, and other recorded mechanic activities.
   - Every mechanic remains visible even on a day with no recorded sessions.
   - Drill-down exposes underlying unit/task/session start-stop evidence.
   - New admin endpoint: GET /api/admin/productivity?start=...&end=...&mechanic=...
   - Range capped at 62 days to prevent oversized queries.

2. Free AI / OpenRouter
   - OPENROUTER_API_KEY supported server-side.
   - AI_PROVIDER=openrouter forces OpenRouter.
   - AI_PROVIDER=auto prefers OpenRouter and may fall back to OpenAI if configured.
   - OPENROUTER_MODEL defaults to openrouter/free.
   - Browser never receives AI API keys.
   - AI status now reports active provider/model.

3. Multilingual Shop Writing
   - AI detects mechanic input language automatically.
   - Professional English, short invoice, detailed repair, customer-friendly, grammar-only, and Ukrainian output modes.
   - Strong prompt guardrails preserve VINs, unit numbers, part numbers, codes, measurements and uncertainty.
   - AI is prohibited by prompt from inventing work performed, parts, measurements, diagnoses, authorization, or results.
   - Inspection Finding fields now have AI -> Professional English buttons.
   - Work-order Completion Notes / Future Repair Notes now have AI -> Professional English buttons.

4. Parts / Cross Reference Assistant
   - Improved instructions for OEM/cross-reference candidates.
   - Requires uncertainty labels and verification guidance.
   - UI explicitly warns that cross-reference/fitment must be verified before ordering/installing.

5. Productivity AI Summary
   - Admin can request an AI management summary of the selected productivity period.
   - AI is instructed not to infer attendance, idle time, ranking, or productivity percentage from missing data.

KNOWN / INTENTIONAL LIMITATIONS
- Voice transcription still uses OpenAI audio transcription and therefore needs OPENAI_API_KEY. Text AI can run on OpenRouter without OpenAI.
- Free OpenRouter models are capacity/rate limited and model selection may vary when using openrouter/free.
- AI part cross-reference output is advisory; final fitment requires OEM/manufacturer/vendor catalog verification.
- Historical 'other activity' depends on retained mechanic activityHistory records; task repair labor uses PostgreSQL task_time_sessions.
