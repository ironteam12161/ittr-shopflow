ITTR v24.6.5 PROFESSIONAL INVOICE / HISTORY / TAX AUDIT
Date: 2026-09-14

Implemented:
- Labor-first invoice hierarchy retained: each labor operation owns its parts/sublet/fees.
- Labor rate control is one combined control with presets:
  New Client $115, Our Client $110, Old Client $100, Owner $60, Custom.
- First labor operation establishes invoice default labor rate. New labor inherits that default.
- Changing labor 2/3/4 does not change the invoice default or later new-labor default.
- Part # and description both search live ITTR inventory; selection fills part #, description, sell price, internal cost and taxable flag.
- Adding a part saves current invoice lines first, preventing unsaved labor text/rates from disappearing on re-render.
- Invoice totals preview live while tax/rate/fees/discount/line values are edited.
- Tax exemption fields added: exempt flag, E/certificate number, reason. Exempt invoice forces calculated tax to $0 server-side.
- Illinois IDOR online jurisdiction tax lookup added using the official machine-readable current rate file. If IDOR marks the jurisdiction as address-override, ITTR refuses to guess and directs user to official MyTax Illinois address finder.
- Paid invoice correction workflow: Reopen for Correction requires reason and is audited.
- Permanent deletion allowed only for unpaid drafts. Finalized invoices are voided instead to preserve accounting records.
- Unit Vehicle Profile history now includes ITTR invoices in addition to ITTR work orders and Fullbay service history.
- Invoice PDF redesigned with Iron Team logo, labor/part hierarchy, vehicle identity, tax-exempt display, totals and professional footer policy.
- PDF footer: manufacturer parts warranty responsibility + 50-mile wheel/tire fastener torque recheck notice.
- Non-destructive invoice schema migration 028 added.
