ITTR v24.5.5 — PostgreSQL / Smart Receiving Audit

ROOT CAUSE CONFIRMED
Production reported: receiving failed at line_1_stock: operator is not unique.
The failing line_1_stock SQL in v24.5.4 contained:
  inventory_value=$2*$4
Both $2 and $4 are PostgreSQL bind parameters. Without explicit types, PostgreSQL can see unknown * unknown and cannot choose a unique multiplication operator.

FIX
- quantity=$2::numeric
- last_purchase_cost=$3::numeric
- cost=$4::numeric
- vendor uses $5::text
- inventory_value=($2::numeric*$4::numeric)
- id=$1::bigint
