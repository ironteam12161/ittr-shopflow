ITTR ShopFlow v24.12.0 Targeted Invoice Refactor

Implemented on top of v24.11.0 to preserve prior fixes.

DATA / API
- Added additive customer_invoices fields: dot_number, discount_type, discount_value, billing address snapshot fields.
- Existing invoice discount values are backfilled as fixed-dollar discounts.
- Invoice PUT now persists customer_id, unit_id, VIN, DOT, mileage, payment terms and global discount mode/value.
- Server calculation taxes only invoice lines marked taxable.
- Global discount supports fixed USD or percent and reduces the taxable basis proportionally.

UI
- Customer/vehicle billing header with Customer and Unit dropdowns.
- Selecting a customer loads its fleet; selecting a unit fills VIN and mileage; customer fills USDOT.
- Controlled payment terms automatically calculate due date.
- Separate Labor & Services and Parts & Materials tables with dedicated subtotals.
- Live subtotal, discount, tax, grand total and balance preview.
- Print stylesheet hides application controls and renders customer-facing billing layout.

COMPATIBILITY
- Existing historical invoices remain readable.
- Paid and void invoice delete visibility fix from v24.11 remains preserved for owner/admin.
- Fullbay v23.7.1 inventory SQL parameter regression guard remains preserved.
