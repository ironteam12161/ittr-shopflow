ITTR v24.6.5 Vehicle History / Invoice Search Audit

FIXED
- Vehicle Profile API now includes ITTR customer invoices in unit history.
- Invoice history includes every invoice line (labor descriptions, job names, part descriptions/numbers) for search.
- Vehicle History search now searches invoice line content, not only invoice header/number.
- Added ITTR Invoices history-source filter.
- Invoice history rows show actual Work Performed summary from invoice lines.
- Invoice rows Open directly in the ITTR invoice editor using the existing /api/invoices/:id route.
- Fullbay rows still open Fullbay service-order detail.
- ITTR WO rows still open work-order detail.
- Changed empty state from service orders to service history.

WHY THE BUG EXISTED
The Vehicle History client filter only searched top-level fields plus vehicleHistorySummary(). ITTR invoice records were not being returned by /api/customer-units/:id/profile in v24.6.4, so invoice labor/part descriptions could not be searched. A row could be displayed by later frontend work but without searchable line detail / correct open routing, causing the observed behavior and endpoint mismatch.
