ShopFlow on your phone

Install the app once. It opens straight to ShopFlow — no browser bar, and you stay logged in.

🤖 Android

Download the Android app
  1. Tap Download, then open ITTR-ShopFlow.apk when it finishes.
  2. If the phone asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then tap Install.
  3. Open ITTR ShopFlow from your home screen and log in.
  4. Allow camera (barcode scanner, photos) and microphone (voice notes) when asked.

Website updates show up in the app automatically — no need to reinstall.

🍎 iPhone

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Open ShopFlow from the home screen — it runs full screen like an app.

Or keep using it in the browser: open ShopFlow
