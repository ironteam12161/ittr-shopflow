ITTR ShopFlow v24.13.0 — Service-Card Invoice Workflow Audit

AUDIT FINDINGS
- v24.12.0 flattened billing into separate Labor and Parts tables. That removed the job/service relationship that existed in v24.6.4/v24.6.6.
- The earlier rate presets were confirmed in v24.6.6 and restored exactly: New client $115/hr, Our client $110/hr, Old client $100/hr, Owner $60/hr, Custom.
- Customer records already retain default_labor_rate; the invoice module now uses that rate when available, otherwise the first existing labor rate, otherwise $115.
- Existing parent_line_id data model remains intact, so no destructive migration is required.

NEW WORKFLOW
1. Create Service / Repair Job.
2. Add Parts & Materials directly under that service.
3. Add one or more Labor rows below the parts.
4. Pick a restored rate preset or type a custom labor rate.
5. Per-line taxable and discount controls remain available.
6. Existing customer/unit autofill, terms, global discount, PDF/print, payment, delete, AI, Fullbay and mobile protections remain.

DATA SAFETY
- No database reset.
- No destructive schema migration.
- Existing invoice lines continue to render by job_name/job_uid and parent_line_id.
