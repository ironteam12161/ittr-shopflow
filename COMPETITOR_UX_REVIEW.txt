ITTR v24.0.0 UX direction

Patterns applied from modern shop-management products:
- Unit-centric record: customer + asset + service history stay connected.
- Universal search instead of separate duplicate lookup screens.
- One primary repair workflow with completed history reachable contextually.
- Real-time shop/team operations grouped together rather than scattered across top-level navigation.
- Daily-use screens stay prominent; diagnostics/import/admin utilities are secondary.

This release intentionally keeps ITTR heavy-duty specific instead of copying passenger-car workflows literally.
