# ITTR ShopFlow AI review and v24.42.0 AI center (2026-10-10)

## 1. What the AI does today, and what it costs

ShopFlow uses OpenRouter. Text and invoice reading use `google/gemini-2.5-flash-lite`, which costs about $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.40 per million output tokens. When a scan fails, it falls back to `gemini-2.5-flash`. Below is a typical cost per use with these models.

| Feature | Where | Typical cost per use | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Screen translation (EN→UA) | Every screen while **AI translate** is on | about $0.0002 per batch of 30 phrases | Fixed in v24.41.9 (no jumping, bad answers rejected). **v24.42.0:** translations are now saved for the whole shop. Each phrase is paid for once, not once per phone, and costs nothing after that. |
| Workshop AI chat | ✦ button | $0.001–0.005 per question (1–5 lookups) | Good design: it looks up only what it needs, and mechanics never see prices. **Added:** a *vendor bills* lookup ("how much did we spend at FleetPride this month?"). |
| Vendor invoice scan | Parts → Smart Receiving, and the new Reports → Vendors | $0.001–0.003 per bill ($0.005–0.01 if the fallback model is needed) | Works. **Added:** due date and terms are read, and it is told never to take your own shop as the vendor. |
| Notes, diagnostics, parts help, report summary | Operations → AI tools, Reports | under $0.001 each | Fine. |
| Workshop manuals | Chat questions about torque, wiring and procedures | $0.002–0.01 when it reads 1–6 pages | The old fallback path sent **the whole manual PDF** (up to 18 MB) with one question, which could cost several cents each time. It is now capped at 8 MB. The normal path reads only the matching pages. |
| Voice notes | Microphone | about $0.006 per minute (OpenAI) | Only if `OPENAI_API_KEY` is set. |

**Conclusion:** normal use by one shop should cost a few dollars a month. Until now nothing measured it, and nothing could stop a mistake (for example a loop or a huge manual) from running up the bill. v24.42.0 adds both.

## 2. New in v24.42.0

### AI usage and monthly budget (Reports → AI usage)
- Every AI call is recorded: the feature, the person, the model, the tokens and the **real cost reported by OpenRouter**. Costs are estimated only when the provider does not report them.
- The page shows this month, a forecast for the month, last month, cost by day, by feature, by person and by model, and recent errors.
- **Monthly budget** (default $25, set by the owner). When the budget is reached, new AI calls stop with a clear message until next month or until the budget is raised. Translations already saved keep working, and nothing else in ShopFlow is affected.

### Vendor bills by vendor (Reports → Vendors)
- **One list of all vendor bills:**
  - parts received with Smart Receiving
  - bills uploaded on this page (PDFs or photos; several at once; AI reads them and you check before saving)
  - vendor-bill emails: Money inbox → Vendor bills → **✦ Scan & file**
- **Spelling variants are merged.** "FleetPride Inc." and "FleetPride" go under one vendor.
- **Per vendor:**
  - spend and share, average bill and last bill
  - unpaid and overdue bills, cores owed
  - top parts
  - monthly chart
  - every bill, with its lines and the original file (when photo storage is set up)
  - **Mark paid**, with a check or ACH number
- **Things to check** (calculated by ShopFlow itself, no AI cost):
  - **Price increases:** the same part from the same vendor costs at least 5% more than last time, and how much extra you paid.
  - **Cheaper at another vendor:** you paid more than another vendor charged you for the same part number in the 180 days before, and what you could have saved.
  - **Bills that don't add up:** lines ≠ subtotal, subtotal + tax + freight ≠ total, or quantity × price ≠ line total.
  - **Possible double bills:** the same invoice number written differently, or the same total within 7 days.
  - **Unpaid bills past due** and **cores past due**.
- **✦ AI summary:** a short written report for the owner based on these numbers (one cheap call).
- Saving the same vendor and invoice number twice is blocked. If a bill was filed first and the parts are later received with Smart Receiving, the received bill replaces the filed one and keeps its file and payment.
- *Owed to vendors* counts bills added here, and received bills once they have a due date. Older Smart Receiving bills never recorded payment, so they are not counted as owed.

### Invoice check (✓ Check invoice, in the invoice editor)
- **Rules** (instant, no AI cost). Before you send an invoice, ShopFlow compares it with the work order and your history:
  - parts used on the work order that are not billed, or billed short
  - mechanic time (job timers plus inspection) that is more than the billed hours, with the dollar value
  - a labor line well below what your shop usually bills for that job type (from Reports → Labor times), with a one-click **Set X h**
  - lines at $0, parts below cost or with a markup under 15%
  - missing unit, VIN or mileage, and no customer email
- **✦ Better wording.** AI rewrites labor descriptions into clear, professional English, translating Ukrainian notes and using only facts from the line and the work order. You pick each suggestion with **Use this**, and nothing changes on its own.

## 3. Tests
`ai_center_integration_test.mjs` runs the real server against a fake OpenRouter, so no credit is used. It checks:
- translation batching and the shared cache
- vendor scan, filing, duplicate block, analysis alerts, paid/overdue, search and AI summary
- invoice check and wording
- usage logging with real cost, by feature and person
- that the budget stops new calls

## 4. Recommended settings
- Leave the budget at **$25/month** at first and look at Reports → AI usage after a week. Most shops will spend well under $10.
- To get the original vendor PDFs in **Open file**, make sure photo storage (R2) is set up in Railway. It is the same storage used for finding photos.
- Add your vendors' email domains to the Gmail vendor search (Money inbox → *What to look for*), for example `from:fleetpride.com`, so more bills arrive for **Scan & file**.
