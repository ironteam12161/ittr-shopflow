## 2. Found but not changed (recommended next steps)

1. **Stripe "Pay online" links are never reconciled.** There is no Stripe webhook, so when a customer pays online the invoice stays unpaid until someone records the payment by hand. Stripe Checkout sessions also expire after 24 hours, so the "View & Pay" button in an older email stops working. Recommended: add a `checkout.session.completed` webhook (with `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`), and generate the link when the email is sent.
2. **Emailing an invoice does not mark it "sent".** Only `email_sent_at` is recorded. A draft you email stays a draft (and is not counted in A/R) until you click Finalize.
3. **Resend before the domain is verified:** Resend only delivers to the account owner's own address, and every other recipient fails. The app now says this plainly. After you verify the domain, set `INVOICE_FROM_EMAIL` to an address on that domain.
4. **Repository clutter.** This is a maintenance risk, not a runtime risk (only `public/` is served):
   - The root `index.html`, `sw.js` and `modules/*` are required mirrors.
   - Root `invoices.html`, `invoices.js`, `customers.*`, `parts.*`, `procenter.*` and `trucksearch.*` are stale copies that differ from `public/modules`.
   - `frontend_check.js`, `frontend_check_2471.js` and `frontend_check_2481.js` are about 1 MB of dead snapshots.
   - There are nine one-off `deep_release_audit_*` and `samsara_*` scripts that CI does not run.
   - SQL migrations are duplicated between the root and `database/migrations`, and there are two different `028_*.sql` files.
5. **Size.** `server.js` is about 470 KB and `index.html` about 600 KB, with many stacked CSS "patch" blocks. New features in this release live in separate files (`finance_center.mjs`, `public/modules/estimates.*`, `public/modules/reports.*`, `public/finance-shared.js`) so they don't make that worse.
6. **Managers cannot act on mechanic tasks.** Managers can't add parts to, or start or stop, a mechanic's task (`role!=="admin"` checks in the task action and task parts routes). This may be intentional. Flagged in case it isn't.
7. **The green build banner** at the top of every page ("ITTR v24.x ONLINE · backend …") is useful for checking deploys but looks like debug output to customers. Consider showing it only to the owner.

## 3. New in v24.37.0

### Navigation
The desktop navigation is now a single full-width tab strip grouped by workflow:
**Dashboard · Work Orders · Findings | MONEY: Estimates · Invoices · Reports | RECORDS: Customers & Vehicles · Parts | Operations.**
On phones, Estimates and Reports are in the **More** sheet.

### Estimates tab
- Create an estimate from a unit, customer, VIN, plate or DOT lookup, or type in a new customer.
- Add jobs (labor), with parts from inventory or custom parts underneath, plus other charges, tax, shop supplies, discount and notes.
- Statuses: Draft → Sent → Approved / Declined. A Sent estimate past its valid-until date shows as Expired.
- **Convert to invoice** creates a draft invoice with the same jobs, parts and fees, keeping each part under its job. You can only convert once.
- PDF uses the same branded layout as invoices, titled ESTIMATE.

### Tire fees
- The **Tire Fees** button on invoices and estimates asks for "new tires sold" and "other tires disposed". It suggests the new-tire count from tire part lines, which you confirm.
- A new tire gets the **tire user fee + disposal fee**. Other tires get the **disposal fee** only.
- Pressing it again updates the same lines instead of adding duplicates.
- Amounts are set by the owner in **Reports → Tire fees**. Defaults: user fee $2.50, retained allowance $0.10 per tire, disposal fee $5.00, not taxable. **Confirm the current Illinois amounts and your disposal price with your accountant.**
- Reports show tires sold, user fee collected, the allowance you keep, the **user fee to send to the state**, and disposal fees, by month.

### Reports tab (for the accountant)
Date presets (this month, last month, quarter, year to date, last year, custom), **Print / Save PDF** (prints every section) and **Export CSV** on every table.
- **Overview:** billed, collected, customers owe (with overdue), parts profit, tire fees, parts purchased. Charts for sales by month (labor, parts, fees) and billed vs. collected, plus revenue mix, payment methods, top customers and the estimate pipeline.
- **Customers owe:** balance per customer, aging buckets (not due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days) and every open invoice. Click one to open it.
- **Parts & spending:** parts purchased by month, spend per vendor, each vendor bill, and every part billed with times used, qty, your cost, sold price and profit.
- **Tire fees:** as described above.
- **Money inbox (Gmail):** see below.

Numbers count only finalized invoices (Sent / Partial / Paid). Drafts and voids are excluded, and drafts are called out separately.

### Gmail money inbox (read-only)
Connect one or more Gmail accounts (owner only). **Check email now** scans for:
- **Zelle received / sent:** amount, sender, memo. For each received Zelle payment ITTR suggests matching open invoices (invoice number in the memo, amount equal to the balance, sender name matching the customer). **Apply** records a `Zelle` payment on that invoice, and it can't be applied twice.
- **Vendor bills:** vendor, amount, invoice number, and PDF attachments you can open.

Items can be marked Reviewed or Ignored. The Gmail search terms are editable, so you can add senders like `from:fleetpride.com`. Refresh tokens are stored AES-256-GCM encrypted. The OAuth `state` is single-use and expires after 10 minutes.

## 4. Setup checklist

### Resend (when you're ready)
Follow the README "Resend invoice email" section: verify the domain (SPF and DKIM), then set `RESEND_API_KEY`, `INVOICE_FROM_EMAIL` and `INVOICE_REPLY_TO`.

### Gmail
1. Go to Google Cloud Console → new project → **APIs & Services → Enable APIs** → enable **Gmail API**.
2. **OAuth consent screen:**
   - If the mailbox is Google Workspace, choose **Internal**. That's the easiest option.
   - For a personal @gmail.com, choose **External**, add your address as a test user, then **Publish app → In production**. Otherwise Google expires the connection every 7 days. Google will show an "unverified app" warning; click *Advanced → Continue*.
3. **Credentials → Create OAuth client ID → Web application.** Authorized redirect URI: `https://<your-railway-domain>/api/gmail/oauth/callback`.
4. In Railway set:
   - `GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`
   - `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`
   - `APP_PUBLIC_URL=https://<your-railway-domain>`
   - `GMAIL_TOKEN_KEY` (any long random text; keep it the same forever, or you'll have to reconnect)
5. Redeploy, then go to **Reports → Money inbox → Connect Gmail**. Repeat for each Gmail account.
