## 4. New in v24.41.0

### Time clock (`time_clock.mjs`)

**What the mechanic sees**
- A big **Clock In / Clock Out** card on *My Work Orders* shows today's and this week's hours, plus overtime once they pass 40 h.
- Clocking out pauses any running job timer and ends the current activity, after a confirmation.
- If a mechanic starts a job timer or an activity while clocked out, they are clocked in automatically. The shift is marked *Auto clock-in*, and the owner can turn this off.

**Safeguards**
- A mechanic can have only one open shift; the database enforces this.
- Shifts can't overlap.
- A forgotten clock-out is closed at the mechanic's last recorded work or at shop closing time, never more than 14 h after clock-in. It is flagged *Forgot to clock out*.

**Reports → Time clock** (owner and managers)
- Hours by week, with overtime past 40 h a week, and hours by day.
- Every punch, with flags.
- Owner-only actions: *Add missed shift*, *Edit* and *Delete*. Each one needs a reason and is kept in the shift's history (`mechanic_shift_edits`). Deleted shifts are kept, not erased.
- Settings: auto clock-in on or off, overtime threshold, week start (Mon or Sun) and the forgotten-shift limit.
- Times are shown and edited in the shop's time zone.

**Productivity report**
- Each day is now measured from clock-in to clock-out instead of fixed shop hours: repair, other work, break and idle *inside the paid shift*.
- Repair time outside the shift is shown as *off the clock*.
- Days without punches still fall back to shop hours.

**Start fresh:** the reset now also clears time-clock data under "Mechanic productivity".

### Labor times (`labor_times.mjs`, Reports → Labor times)

**Sources**
- Invoice labor lines: billed hours.
- Task timers: the real time spent, and by whom.
- Finished work-order tasks that aren't invoiced yet.
- Imported Fullbay history: billed hours per tech. Fullbay tech names are matched to ShopFlow mechanics by name.

**Job types**
- Each job is sorted into one of 26 built-in types by keywords (wheel seal, PM / lube, king pins, brakes, brake chamber, slack adjuster, air dryer, suspension and others).
- The owner can rename types, add keywords, reorder them and set a standard ("book") time.
- Descriptions that match no type are listed, so a keyword can be added.

**Per job type**
- Typical billed hours (the median), the usual range (the middle half of jobs) and the typical real time from timers.

**Per mechanic and job type**
- Median real time, and how it compares with the shop's typical real time for the same job (−20% = faster).
- **Billed ÷ real** shows whether the hours charged cover the time spent.
- Mechanics are compared with the shop's real time, not with billed hours, because billed hours are often flat-rate or padded. Fullbay jobs are compared only with other Fullbay jobs.
- A job counts for one mechanic only when that mechanic did at least 80% of the timer time. Otherwise it counts as a team job.

**Drill-down:** click a job type to see each mechanic, and every job behind the numbers with links to the invoice or work order. CSV export is available everywhere.

### Tips for useful labor numbers

- **Mechanics must use the job timers** (Start / Pause / Complete). Real-time comparisons only exist for timed jobs.
- **Give each labor line one job.** "Brake and PM service" counts only as a PM job, because the first match wins.
- **Import Fullbay history** (see `FULLBAY_HISTORY_IMPORT.md`) to start with years of typical times.
